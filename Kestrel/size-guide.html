Size Guide

How to measure

Use a soft tape, keep it level, and measure over a thin layer — not bare skin, not a jumper.

  • Chest — around the fullest part, under the arms, tape flat across the back.
  • Natural waist — the narrowest point of your torso, usually just above the navel.
  • Hip — around the fullest part of your seat, feet together.
  • Inseam — from the crotch seam to the hem of a pair of trousers that fit you well.
  • Sleeve — from the centre back of your neck, over the shoulder, to the wrist bone.

Fit notes

The Camden Overshirt

Cut long and roomy to layer over knitwear. If you want it as a shirt, size down.

Heavyweight Tee

A boxy, structured body with a slightly shorter sleeve. Not slim.

Balmacaan Wool Coat

Designed to sit over a jacket. Take your normal size; it isn't meant to be close.

Pleated Wide Trouser

A high, true waist and a full leg. It breaks once on the shoe at the length shown.
