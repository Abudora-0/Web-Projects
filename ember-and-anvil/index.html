The Craft

Five Fires, One Edge

A blade passes through the same five stages it has for three thousand years. We haven't found a reason to skip any of them.

  1. 01

    Heat

    Raw high-carbon billet buried in coal until it glows a color no swatch book owns - somewhere past cherry, short of the sun.

  2. 02

    Hammer

    Hundreds of strikes draw the steel out and pack the grain tight. Every hammer face leaves a signature you can still feel under your thumb.

  3. 03

    Quench

    A plunge into oil at exactly the wrong moment ruins a blade forever. At exactly the right one, it locks the hardness in for good.

  4. 04

    Temper

    Back into a gentler heat to trade a little hardness for a lot of resilience - straw, then bronze, then purple, then blue, read straight off the steel.

  5. 05

    Hone

    Stone after stone after stone, by hand, until the edge disappears when you look at it edge-on. That's how you know it's done.
