      Our Craft

      From hive to jar

      Five unhurried steps, the same way our grandparents did it - just with better boots.

      1. 01

        Tend the hives

        Our beekeepers walk the meadow weekly, checking brood patterns and making sure every queen is thriving before a single frame is touched.

      2. 02

        Harvest the frames

        When the comb is capped in wax, the honey is ripe. Frames are lifted gently, by hand, never rushed and never over-taken from the colony.

      3. 03

        Uncap & spin

        Wax caps are sliced away and frames spun in a hand-cranked centrifuge that coaxes the honey free without a single degree of heat.

      4. 04

        Strain & settle

        Raw honey rests in settling tanks, filtered just enough to remove wax - nothing else. No pasteurizing, no additives, ever.

      5. 05

        Jar & label

        Each jar is hand-filled and labeled with its harvest date and hive number, then set aside for a home on your table.
